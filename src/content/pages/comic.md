Carina Wedding is an apprentice seamstress who designs outfits for her very favorite people in the world: magical girls. Pressured into accepting that her dreams of harnessing her own magic are 'unrealistic', she contentedly brings her passion to life in other ways... Or so she says. But when an old friend barrels into her life with a Catalyst in her arms, Carina is given the opportunity to make those long-abandoned dreams a reality.

RECATALYZED is a magical girl comic about growing up and reacquainting yourself with the person you want to become.
