caption: |-
  An illustration featuring Gil, a lithe, sharply-dressed man with short black hair, and Eve, a tall woman with princess-like attire and knee-length blonde pigtails. Both take hold of opposite ends of a notched bow and arrow. Gil has one hand in his pocket and the other on the bow. He leans back, drawing the bowstring and aiming the arrow towards his heart. He smiles easily.
  Eve, conversely, has a tense hand on the arrow and bowstring. Her free hand falls behind her back. Her stance is rigid, and her expression lands somewhere between affronted and concerned.
  This piece is based on one of my favorite pieces of performance art, "Rest Energy" (1980) performed by Marina Abramović and Ulay.
