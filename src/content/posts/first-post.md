It's a little funny -- almost exactly a year ago, I was writing up my first blogpost on my old strawpage. I got a fly in my ear about wanting a space on the internet that was really mine, and so I spent about a week and a half fussily putting together a facsimile of old internet sensibilities that I wasn't really around for. I still think it's a darling little site, but alas... I must retire it.

So, get this. I've had two websites all this time. My neocities, which I hastily threw together because my current employers asked me for a portfolio I didn't have (thank you guys for taking a chance on me!!!), and my strawpage, which I intended to be more of a blog/hub for all my various ventures. And I was spending money on both of them despite using neither of them!! Aaaaaagh!!

Whatever. They're good sites, they can have some of my picture-drawing money. I'm more upset by how long it took for me to migrate than anything else.

So, hi! This is my website. For real this time! This is where I'm going to archive the art I make, the sketchbooks I fill, and all the fun little sidequests I go on. 

My dear darling friend Rosematcha taught me everything I needed to know about being a webmaster (am I a poser for calling myself that? I feel like one...!) and did most of the work putting this thing together for me. She's an angel and I love her terribly. Thank you so much, Reese!!

That's right, it has a name! Finally. After many months of alluding to it vaguely, I'm happy to present RECATALYZED in its newly christened form. It'll be a while until I'm ready to start posting pages--likely at some point in 2027. I look forward to properly introducing you all to this world and these characters, so please look forward to meeting them!

My next project is updating my ko-fi. However, that's actually just a project-rock that, when lifted, reveals a ton of project-bugs beneath it. The end goal is to set up memberships in case any of y'all want to support me monthly, with various \~cool benefits\~ for doing so. Most of the rewards will be free eventually -- for example, the scan of the last sketchbook I finished -- but the entire reason I'm doing this is because I want to set up a sticker shop and a mailing list. So you, yes, you, can put art made by your local apple guy in various locations. 

I also have a stream I want to do to commemorate all that that I'm working on. Things are busy in Luci land!

I'll scream it from the heavens whenever all that is squared away. In the meantime, thanks for being patient with me, and for enjoying my art. It means a lot.

This is Luci, signing off! Take care!

PS: Archiving my art for this site was an EXPERIENCE. I have a bunch of older art I _could_ put on here, but... I think that has to be for me. For my sanity.
